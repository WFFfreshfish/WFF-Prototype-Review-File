Resource Rankings

The Lake Rankings provide anglers with a simple way to compare Alabama’s public fisheries based on the qualities that matter most on the water. Rather than relying on a single measurement, each lake is evaluated using information collected during standardized fisheries surveys, including how many fish are caught, the number of quality-size fish available, fish condition, and growth rates. These measurements are converted into standardized scores so they can be compared fairly across lakes, then combined into rankings based on different angler preferences. Whether your goal is to catch lots of fish, target trophy-sized fish, or find a balance between the two, the rankings can help identify lakes that best match your fishing style. To provide a more reliable picture of each fishery, rankings are based on the average score from all years of available survey data rather than the results of a single sampling year. Although fishing success can vary with weather, season, and other environmental conditions, these rankings offer a consistent, science-based comparison of Alabama’s fisheries using long-term monitoring data collected by ADCNR biologists.
